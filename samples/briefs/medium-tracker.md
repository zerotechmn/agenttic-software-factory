# Brief: Hackathon Team Tracker

Organizers of a 24-hour hackathon need a web app to keep track of teams and their progress.

## Needs
- Register teams: team name, members (names), and project idea.
- Each team has a status that moves through: Registered → Building → Submitted → Judged.
- Organizers post short progress updates per team (timestamped).
- A dashboard shows how many teams are in each status, and a live list of the latest updates across all teams.
- Judges can score a submitted team from 1 to 10 on three criteria (Innovation, Execution, Demo) with an optional comment.
- A leaderboard ranks judged teams by average score.
- Filter teams by status and search by team or member name.

## Notes
- Single organizer-facing app, no login required for the demo.
- Should come with realistic demo data so it looks alive on first load.
- Must work well on a laptop screen and be usable on a phone.
